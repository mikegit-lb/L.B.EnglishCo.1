Section 02

Privacy & KVKK disclosure framework

This is the site-wide privacy framework. A short, activity-specific notice should also appear beside every live form at the moment personal data is collected.

i
Current prototype behaviour

The consultation form in the local prototype demonstrates the experience but does not transmit entries. The live form provider, recipient inbox, retention period and international-transfer position must be added before collection begins.

Data that may be collected

  • Identity and contact data, such as name, email address and telephone number.
  • Learning information, such as current level, exam goal, target score, preferred format, timetable and messages voluntarily submitted.
  • Booking and transaction information when a scheduling or payment service is connected.
  • Technical and security information generated by the hosting, form or payment provider.

Purposes

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange consultation or teaching services.
  • Assess programme fit and prepare an appropriate learning recommendation.
  • Deliver purchased services or digital products, provide support and maintain required records.
  • Protect the website, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.
  • Send marketing only when there is a valid basis and any required separate permission.

Legal basis, recipients and transfers

The final notice must identify the actual legal basis relied upon for each purpose, the categories of recipients, the collection method and any overseas transfer. Likely processors may include hosting, form, email, scheduling, video-class, cloud-storage and payment providers, but none should be listed until the real production stack is chosen and contracted.

Retention and rights

Personal data should be retained only for the documented business or legal period, then deleted or anonymised securely. The final notice must explain how a data subject can exercise applicable rights—including the rights set out under Article 11 of Türkiye’s Law No. 6698—and where to send a request.

Do not use this general page as the only form notice

At the point of collection, provide a concise notice that clearly identifies the controller, purpose, recipients, collection method/legal basis and rights route for that specific activity.

Section 03

Cookies & browser storage

Use the smallest amount of browser storage needed, explain it plainly, and do not load optional analytics or advertising tools before valid consent where consent is required.

Current local site

  • No advertising, behavioural profiling or analytics cookies are intentionally set by this prototype.
  • The main site uses local browser storage to remember saved resource cards.
  • The free library uses local browser storage to remember saved resources, checklist progress and notes. This information stays in that browser and is not sent to L.B. English Co.
  • Google Fonts is requested from Google’s servers in the present design; the production privacy assessment should account for that external request or self-host the fonts.

Future tools

If analytics, marketing pixels, embedded media or other non-essential technologies are added, update this notice and implement a genuine choice before those technologies run. The control should make accepting and rejecting equally understandable and allow the user to change the choice later.

You can remove local resource data through your browser’s site-data controls. Clearing it removes saved cards, notes and checklist progress from that browser.

Section 04

Terms of service

These terms establish the baseline for consultations, private coaching, VIP groups and speaking sessions. The commercial details shown before booking must take priority where they are more specific.

Service formation

  • Website descriptions and availability information are invitations to enquire, not a guaranteed place.
  • A booking is confirmed only after the learner receives written confirmation and completes any required payment.
  • The confirmation should state the programme, tutor, format, timetable, price, payment schedule, included materials and access period.

Attendance, rescheduling and cancellation

Business decision required

Add the real notice period, late-cancellation treatment, tutor-cancellation remedy, group-class rule, package expiry and exceptional-circumstances process. Show these terms before payment.

Learner responsibilities

  • Provide accurate level, goal and booking information.
  • Join with suitable internet, audio/video equipment and a respectful learning environment.
  • Use materials only for personal learning and do not record, reproduce, share or resell lessons or resources without written permission.
  • Respect the privacy and participation of other learners in VIP groups and speaking rooms.

Minors

If services are offered to learners under the applicable age of legal capacity, obtain the required parent or guardian involvement, adapt communications and privacy information, and confirm the payment/contracting party.

Liability and availability

L.B. English Co. should provide services with reasonable care and skill. Nothing in the final terms should exclude rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded. Online services may occasionally be affected by third-party platform, internet or device interruptions; the final policy should state the fair remedy offered when L.B. English Co. cannot deliver a confirmed session.

Section 05

Digital products, distance sales & refunds

Premium products may combine webpages, webapps, PDFs, slides, editable documents, audio or video. Each product page and checkout must describe the complete learning package—not merely a file count.

Information to show before payment

  • Provider identity and contact details.
  • Core product characteristics, learning purpose, included formats and technical requirements.
  • Total price, taxes/fees, payment method, delivery method, access start and access duration.
  • Support channel, complaint route, cancellation/withdrawal information and any applicable exception.
  • Licence limits, device/account conditions and whether updates are included.

Personal-use licence

Unless a separate educator or institutional licence says otherwise, premium content should be licensed to one purchaser for personal learning. Copyright remains with L.B. English Co. or the identified creator. Access does not transfer ownership or permit sharing, resale, public display, extraction or derivative commercial use.

Final refund policy must be set before checkout

Do not publish a generic “no refunds” statement. Confirm the rules that apply to services, instantly supplied digital content, defective access, duplicate payment and consumer withdrawal rights in the relevant jurisdiction. Record any legally required acknowledgement before beginning immediate digital supply.

Faults and support

If a purchased resource cannot be accessed or materially differs from its description, the learner should be able to contact support for restoration, replacement or another remedy required by law. Add the live support email and response window before sales begin.

Section 06

Education, results & exam independence

Clear expectations protect both the learner and the credibility of the studio.

  • L.B. English Co. provides education, practice materials and preparation consultancy. It does not issue official exam results, admissions decisions or CEFR certificates unless an explicitly identified authorised assessment is separately offered.
  • No particular score, band, admission outcome, promotion or level improvement is guaranteed. Results depend on the learner’s starting point, attendance, independent work, response to feedback and performance conditions.
  • IELTS, TOEFL, SAT, YDT, YDS and YÖKDİL names are used descriptively. The corresponding marks and examination systems belong to their respective owners.
  • L.B. English Co. is independent and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by those exam owners unless a future page expressly documents a genuine authorised relationship.
  • Free and premium practice materials should be original or properly licensed and should be described as practice—not official examination content.
