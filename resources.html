Opinion (Agree/Disagree) Structure

Introduction (40-45 words):
• Paraphrase the prompt statement using academic synonyms.
• Thesis statement: "This essay completely agrees/disagrees with this notion because [Reason A] and [Reason B]."
Body Paragraph 1 & 2:
• Topic sentence stating primary claim clearly.
• Explanation sentence: "This is largely due to the fact that..."
• Concrete illustrative example: "A prominent case in point is..."
• Concluding outcome sentence: "Consequently, it is evident that..."
Band 8+ Cohesive Connectors:
Predominantly · Invariably · Compelling evidence · Far-reaching ramifications
