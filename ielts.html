Part 2 Speaking Cue Card Drill

Click to draw a genuine IELTS speaking cue card. Use the 1-minute planning timer, then deliver your 2-minute talk with structured cohesion.

Topic: Technology & Society

Describe an electronic device that you find indispensable.

You should say:

  • What device it is and when you acquired it
  • How often and for what purposes you use it
  • How it has changed your daily routine or productivity
  • And explain why you would find it difficult to live without it.
⏱️ Planning Timer (60s):
60s
1. The "PPF" Tense Expansion Trick

Don't stay in the present tense. Talk about the Past (how you used to manage before), the Present (your current routine), and the Future (how you anticipate it evolving).

2. High-Yield Cohesive Signposts

Replace "and then" with "Consequently," "From a personal standpoint," "What struck me most was," and "In hindsight."

3. Idiomatic & Collocational Precision

Score Band 8 in Lexical Resource by using natural collocations: "seamless integration," "drastically streamline," "second nature," and "vital lifeline."
